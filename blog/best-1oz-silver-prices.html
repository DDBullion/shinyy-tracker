Why 1 oz Silver is the Most Popular Size

One-ounce silver coins and bars are the standard unit in the bullion market. They offer the best balance of liquidity, recognizability, and affordability. Whether you're buying American Silver Eagles, Canadian Maple Leafs, or generic rounds, the 1 oz format is the most widely traded.

Premiums on 1 oz silver typically run between $2 and $6 over spot price, depending on the product type and dealer. Coins like American Silver Eagles carry the highest premiums due to their legal tender status and collector demand. Generic silver rounds from private mints offer the lowest premiums.

1 oz Silver Products to Consider

American Silver Eagles: The most recognized silver coin in the world. Produced by the US Mint, legal tender, and highly liquid. Expect to pay $3–$6 over spot. Great for long-term stacking and easy resale.

Canadian Silver Maple Leafs: Produced by the Royal Canadian Mint with a security feature (micro-engraved maple leaf). Premium is usually $1–$2 lower than Eagles. Very popular with international buyers.

Silver Buffalo Rounds: Generic silver rounds with the classic Buffalo nickel design. No government backing, but .999 fine silver. Usually the cheapest 1 oz silver option at $1–$3 over spot.

Silver Rounds (Generic): Various designs from private mints. The lowest premiums available, typically $1–$2.50 over spot. Best for maximizing ounces per dollar.

Silver Price Outlook

Silver tends to follow gold but with more volatility. Industrial demand for silver in solar panels, electronics, and electric vehicles has been growing steadily. Many investors buy silver both as a precious metals hedge and as an industrial metals play.
