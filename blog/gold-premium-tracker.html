Weekly gold price report — Updated April 2026

The gold premium over spot is what you pay above the raw commodity price when buying physical gold coins and bars. Understanding premiums is essential: two dealers can show the same spot price but charge very different final prices. This tracker helps you find the lowest gold premiums each week.

Quick tip: Gold premiums typically run 2–5% on bars and 4–8% on popular coins like American Gold Eagles. Anything over 10% is expensive unless you're buying fractional coins (1/10 oz, 1/4 oz).

How to Minimize Your Gold Premium

Buy 1 oz over fractional. Fractional gold (1/10, 1/4, 1/2 oz) carries dramatically higher premiums per ounce. A 1/10 oz coin might carry a 15–18% premium vs. 5% on a full 1 oz coin.

Pay with check or ACH. Most dealers charge 3–4% extra for credit card payments. Paying by check or bank transfer can save more than the entire premium difference between dealers.

Buy during promotions. Dealers like SD Bullion and APMEX regularly run flash sales and quantity discounts. Sign up for email alerts and check for promo codes before purchasing.

Watch the gold/silver ratio. When gold is expensive relative to silver (ratio above 80), many stackers shift to silver for better value. When gold pulls back, premiums often dip too.
