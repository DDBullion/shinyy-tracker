About Shinyy Tracker

Shinyy Tracker is a free, independent tool for precious metals buyers. We track live silver and gold prices from top online dealers, show real premiums over spot, and surface eBay sold listings so you always know the true market value before you buy.

We are not affiliated with any dealer. Our goal is simple: help you find the best deal on silver and gold.
